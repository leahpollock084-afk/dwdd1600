Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship means using technology and the internet in a safe, responsible, respectful, and ethical way.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law refers to the rules and laws that govern how people use technology, computers, and the internet.

What is AUP?

An Accetable Use Policy is a set of rules that explains how people are allowed to use computers, networks, the internet, and other technology at a school, workplace, or organization.

What is Plagarism?

Plagiarism is using someone else's words, ideas, or work without giving them credit.

To avoid plagiarism, give credit to the original creator by citing your sources.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty is cheating or using unfair methods to get an advantage in schoolwork.

In short: Academic dishonesty means being dishonest in your schoolwork or assessments.

What are the rules for AI Generated Content?

The rules for AI-generated content depend on your school or teacher, but common rules include be honest about using AI, follow your teacher's instructions about when AI is allowed, don't turn in AI-generated work as your own if your teacher says you must do the work yourself, check AI's information because it can make mistakes, give credit or disclose AI use when required, and don't use AI to cheat on tests, assignments, or other assessments.

Alphabet & Pronunciation

Vowels

  • A = ah, as in manzana (apple)
  • E = eh, as in león (lion)
  • I = ee, as in sí (yes)
  • O = oh, as in oso (bear)
  • U = oo, as in uno (one)

Key Things to Remember

  • The letter H is completely silent, as in huevo (egg). Pronounced oo-eh-vo.
  • The letter J makes an English H sound, as in jalapeño. Pronounced hah-leh-pen-yo.
  • The letter Q makes an English K sound, as in queso (cheese). Pronounced kay-so.

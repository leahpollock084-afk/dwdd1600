Why Learn Spanish?

Did you know that there are 21 countries where Spanish is the official language? Not to mention 519 million native speakers. Being able to communicate with people from all over the world is not only super cool; it allows you to learn more about their cultures, become a safer traveler, and look really good on resumes. According to the University of Cambridge, the benefits of being bilingual also include:

  1. Better academic performance
  2. Improved concentration
  3. A more powerful memory
  4. Stronger communication skills
  5. More creativity

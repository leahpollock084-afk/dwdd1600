Who Were the Signers

Famous Signers

Some of the most famous signers of the Declaration of Independence were:

Thomas Jefferson
Main author of the Declaration; later became the 3rd U.S. president.
John Hancock
President of the Continental Congress; famous for his very large signature.
Benjamin Franklin
Famous scientist, inventor, diplomat, and statesman.
John Adams
Major advocate for independence; later became the 2nd U.S. president.
Samuel Adams
Important revolutionary leader and organizer in Massachusetts.
John Witherspoon
The only active college president to sign; president of the College of New Jersey (now Princeton).
Charles Carroll of Carrollton
One of the wealthiest and most prominent Maryland patriots and the last surviving signer.

Each of these men played a key role in the foundation of the United States of America.
